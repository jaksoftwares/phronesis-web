32. One Important Architectural Decision

Keep the public website and authenticated application conceptually separate, even though they can live in the same frontend codebase if you choose.

PUBLIC EXPERIENCE
phronesis...
│
├── Marketing
├── Institution
├── Academics
├── Resources
├── Admissions
└── Information

AUTHENTICATED EXPERIENCE
│
├── Learner
├── Parent
├── Teacher
└── Administration

The public site establishes identity and trust.

The application delivers learning and operations.

That separation will make the Phronesis platform feel much more like a genuine educational institution rather than one enormous dashboard with a marketing homepage attached.
